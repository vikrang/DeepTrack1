Company logos for investment marks
==================================

DeepTrack ships with logos for the companies in the ledger (banks, AMCs,
insurers, India Post, listed companies and a few crypto coins). Each file
is shown next to that company's holdings in place of the coloured initials.
Logos are trademarks of their owners and are used only to identify the
company. Sources: stock logos come from Groww's public company-logo image
service (an Indian broker), keyed by NSE symbol; banks, insurers and other
issuers use the icon published on the company's own website; a few were
taken from Wikimedia Commons. Swap any file for an official one at any
time.

For stocks and ETFs the file named after the NSE symbol wins, so
hdfcbank.webp covers any holding whose symbol is HDFCBANK. Other holdings
look up by company name: hdfc-bank.png covers "HDFC Bank", "HDFC Bank
Ltd" and "FD - HDFC Bank - 12345" alike.

To add or replace a logo, drop an image here named in lower case with
hyphens instead of spaces, using .png, .svg, .webp, .jpg, .jpeg or .ico.
No restart is needed - the dashboard reads this folder on every load.

  infy.webp            Infosys (stock symbol INFY)
  hdfc-bank.png        HDFC Bank (banks, FDs, PPF)
  icici-bank.svg       ICICI Bank
  sbi.png              SBI / State Bank of India
  bajaj-finance.png    Bajaj Finance
  tanishq.png          Tanishq
  india-post.png       India Post
  utkarshbnk.webp      Utkarsh Small Finance Bank (FDs)
  suryoday-sfb.jpg     Suryoday Small Finance Bank (FDs)
  equitas-sfb.jpg      Equitas Small Finance Bank (FDs)
  slice.jpg            slice (FDs)
  tamil-nadu-power-finance.png   Tamil Nadu Power Finance (FDs)

Names are matched loosely: trailing words like Ltd, Limited, India and
Bank are ignored when looking for a match. Square images around 64x64 to
128x128 work best. Anything without a logo file keeps its letter mark, so
the app never looks broken.
